Welcome

A holiday apartment for families, steps from the center of Reggio Calabria.

This exclusive-use holiday apartment for up to 6 guests is located in a brand-new building, in a quiet and residential area just minutes from the heart of Reggio Calabria.

It features an eat-in kitchen with living area, two bedrooms and two bathrooms, each equipped with every comfort. All rooms have independent heating.

Good neighbours

House Rules

A few simple rules, designed for mutual respect and for those who come after you.

  1. Keep the apartment clean and tidy; dispose of waste following the provided calendar, placing it in front of the electric meters.
  2. Switch off lights and heating when you're away. The apartment is fully smart-home enabled and externally video-monitored; consumption is recorded.
  3. Smoking is not permitted, either inside or outside the apartment.
  4. Please report any damage promptly. A fee of 100 € applies in case of lost keys.
  5. Quiet hours from 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Parties are not allowed.
  6. Check-in after 3:00 PM · Check-out by 10:00 AM.

Medusa Suite

An intimate stay, reserved for one couple at a time.
By the Strait of Messina, where myth meets the sea.

The Medusa Suite is our most exclusive offering: the master bedroom with private en-suite bathroom, plus kitchen, living room and veranda entirely at your disposal. The apartment is yours alone — no other guests, no shared spaces — designed for those seeking a romantic getaway without compromise.

Pure cotton sheets, warm lighting, a bottle of Prosecco waiting for you at check-in, and the freedom to live the home as if it were yours for a few days. The Strait is just steps away, the city center a short walk.

The experience includes

The Area

Steps away from everything.

The apartment is located in a quiet residential neighbourhood right next to the city center, easily reachable on foot or by public transport — the bus stop is less than 100 metres away. Reggio Calabria Centrale station, served by Frecciarossa, Frecciargento and Italo high-speed trains, is a 13-minute walk away: you can arrive by train and do without a car for your entire stay. Ideal for a relaxing stay, yet close to everything. And if you do come by car, private parking in the city center is truly hard to find.
