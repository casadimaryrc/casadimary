2. Data collected and processing purposes

2.1 Booking form

When you fill in the availability request form in the "Contact" section, the data you enter (name, e-mail, phone, check-in/check-out dates, number of guests, optional message and selected experience type) is not stored on this site. When you click the "Send request via WhatsApp" button, your browser opens the WhatsApp application with a pre-filled message containing this data.

The actual sending of the message only happens if you confirm it yourself from the WhatsApp interface. From that moment on, the message and your personal data are processed according to the Privacy Policy of WhatsApp Ireland Ltd., an autonomous controller for the messaging platform.

The controller of Casa di Mary processes the data received via WhatsApp (or by direct e-mail to casadimaryrc@gmail.com) for the following purposes:

  • Reply to your availability request and provide information about the stay (legal basis: art. 6.1.b GDPR — pre-contractual measures taken at the data subject's request).
  • Manage any booking, check-in and legal obligations (art. 6.1.b and 6.1.c GDPR — contractual and legal obligations, including communication to public security authorities under art. 109 TULPS).

The site uses technical cookies and localStorage to remember your preference regarding third-party cookies consent. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to the controller. For more details see the Cookie Policy.
