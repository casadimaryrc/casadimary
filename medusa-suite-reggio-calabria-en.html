The suite

Designed for two.

The Medusa Suite is an apartment all to yourselves in a newly built building, in a quiet residential area of Reggio Calabria: a double bedroom with en-suite bathroom, kitchen and living room, a large veranda and a private parking space right below. No other guests, no shared spaces.

The bedroom is inspired by the myth of Medusa, the Gorgon whose gaze held all who met it: deep colours, warm lights, careful details. The sea is 400 metres away, the station 900 metres, the centre 1.4 km.

It is rated 4.95 out of 5 on Airbnb from 22 reviews. Mary, your host, is a Superhost with 9 years of hosting.

The area

A weekend in Reggio.

The Medusa Suite is on Via Sbarre Inferiori, in the south of the city, in a quiet neighbourhood with a bakery, café, pharmacy and supermarkets right on the doorstep.

  • By plane. Reggio Calabria's Aeroporto dello Stretto is about 4 km away.
