The tournament

World-class tennis in Reggio Calabria.

From 9 to 13 December 2026 the Next Gen ATP Finals are played at the PalaCalafiore in Reggio Calabria: five days of matches and about 8,450 seats. Tickets have been on sale since 25 September on the official tournament website.

If you are coming with friends, family or colleagues, Casa di Mary is a whole apartment for up to 6 guests, with 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms and a private parking space right downstairs. All yours, no shared spaces.

Getting there

From the PalaCalafiore to home.

Casa di Mary is in Via Sbarre Inferiori, in the south of the city. The PalaCalafiore is in Via Nazionale Pentimele, in the north: about 8 km, so it is not walking distance. Here is how to get around.

PalaCalafiore

8km

Central Station

900m

Airport

4km

Motorway

2,5km

  • By car. The easiest option: leave from your private parking space and, late at night, find it free again right downstairs. With thousands of spectators in town, parking in the centre will be hard to find.
  • By taxi. The right choice for evening sessions if you don’t have a car.
  • By train. Ideal for arriving in Reggio: Frecciarossa, Frecciargento and Italo stop at Reggio Calabria Centrale, a 13-minute walk away. We don’t recommend it for getting back from evening matches: the last trains from Pentimele leave early compared with the end of the sessions.
  • By plane. Reggio Calabria Airport (Aeroporto dello Stretto) is about 4 km from the apartment.

Before you arrive we send you practical directions for match nights via WhatsApp. If dedicated shuttle buses are announced, we will add them here.
