Frequently asked questions

Everything you ask before booking

Location and getting here

Is there parking?

Yes. There is a free private parking space below the building at Via Sbarre Inferiori 131, reserved for guests. It suits compact and mid-size cars: if you are travelling with a large car, write to us first and we will advise you.

How far is the Central Station?

900 metres, about a 13-minute walk. Frecciarossa, Frecciargento, Italo and regional trains to Scilla and the Costa Viola leave from there.

How far are the sea and the centre?

The sea is 400 metres away. The centre and the Cathedral are 1.4 km away, about a 20-minute walk. The Archaeological Museum with the Riace Bronzes is 2.5 km away.

How do I get here from the airport?

Reggio Calabria's Aeroporto dello Stretto is about 4 km away, around ten minutes by taxi or car.

And by car, from the motorway?

The motorway exit is 2.5 km away. On arrival your private parking space is waiting below the building.

What is the area like?

It is a quiet residential area in the south of the city, in a newly built building. Right on the doorstep there are a bakery, café, pharmacy, supermarkets and a bus stop.

Is it convenient for the Next Gen ATP Finals?

The PalaCalafiore is about 8 km away, in the north of the city. The easiest option is the car: you leave from and return to your private parking space. All the details are on the tournament page.

The apartments

What is the difference between Casa di Mary and the Medusa Suite?

Casa di Mary is the whole apartment for up to 6 guests, with 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms: designed for families and groups. The Medusa Suite is designed for couples: a double bedroom with en-suite bathroom, kitchen and living room, and a veranda, with a sofa bed for up to 4 guests. Discover the Medusa Suite.

Are there other guests or shared spaces?

No. In both options the spaces you use are yours alone: no other guests, no shared spaces.

How is the kitchen equipped?

Fully: oven, fridge-freezer, dishwasher, air fryer, coffee maker, crockery and pans.

Is there Wi-Fi, heating and a washing machine?

Yes: free Wi-Fi, independent heating, a Smart TV and a washing machine on the veranda with a drying rack.

Is there a lift? Is it accessible?

Yes, the building has a lift and step-free access.

Booking and house rules

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3 pm, check-out by 10 am, for both options.

Can we arrive late in the evening?

Yes. Check-in is self-service with a key lockbox: we send you the instructions before arrival and you can get in even late at night.

Are pets allowed?

No, pets are not allowed.

Is smoking allowed?

No, smoking is not allowed inside or outside the apartment.

Is there a tourist tax?

Yes, the City of Reggio Calabria's tourist tax: €1 per person per night, for a maximum of 5 nights.
